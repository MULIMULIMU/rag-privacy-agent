# Firmware Update Guide

## Keeping Your AcmeIoT Devices Up to Date

Regular firmware updates ensure your devices have the latest features, security patches, and performance improvements.

## Automatic Updates (Recommended)

By default, all AcmeIoT devices are configured to update automatically during low-usage hours (2:00 AM - 4:00 AM local time).

### Verify Auto-Update Settings
1. Open the AcmeIoT Home app
2. Go to **Settings** > **Device Management**
3. Ensure **Automatic Updates** is toggled ON
4. Select preferred update window

## Manual Firmware Update

### Via AcmeIoT Home App

1. Open the AcmeIoT Home app
2. Navigate to **Devices**
3. Select the device you want to update
4. Tap the **Settings** icon
5. Scroll to **Firmware Version**
6. If an update is available, tap **Update Now**
7. Wait for the update to complete (5-10 minutes)
8. Device will restart automatically

### What to Expect During Update

- **SmartRouter**: LED flashes blue/amber, brief internet interruption (3-5 minutes)
- **SmartPlug**: LED flashes green, maintains power state during update
- **SmartCam**: LED flashes red/blue, recording may pause briefly

## Update Requirements

- Stable internet connection required
- Device must have at least 20% power (battery devices)
- Do not unplug device during update
- Keep the AcmeIoT app open during manual updates

## Checking Current Firmware Version

1. Open AcmeIoT Home app
2. Select your device
3. Tap **Settings** > **About**
4. View **Firmware Version** field

## Troubleshooting Update Issues

### Update Failed
- Ensure stable internet connection
- Restart device and retry
- Check for adequate storage/memory

### Update Stuck
- Wait 15 minutes before intervening
- If still stuck, power cycle the device
- Contact support if problem persists

### Device Won't Update
- Verify device is online in app
- Check that device has latest app version
- Try manual update instead of automatic
