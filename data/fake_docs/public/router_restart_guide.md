# AcmeIoT Router Restart Guide

## Overview
This guide provides instructions for properly restarting your AcmeIoT SmartRouter Pro or SmartRouter Lite devices.

## When to Restart Your Router
- Connection issues or slow speeds
- After firmware updates
- Device is unresponsive
- Troubleshooting network problems

## Method 1: Using the AcmeIoT App
1. Open the AcmeIoT Home app on your smartphone
2. Navigate to **Devices** > **Routers**
3. Select your router from the list
4. Tap the **Settings** icon (gear symbol)
5. Scroll down and tap **Restart Router**
6. Confirm by tapping **Restart Now**
7. Wait 60-90 seconds for the router to complete the restart cycle

## Method 2: Using the Physical Button
1. Locate the power button on the back of your router
2. Press and hold for 2 seconds until the LED flashes amber
3. Release the button
4. Wait for the LED to turn solid blue (approximately 60-90 seconds)

## Method 3: Power Cycle
1. Unplug the power cable from the router
2. Wait 30 seconds
3. Plug the power cable back in
4. Wait 60-90 seconds for the router to fully boot

## What to Expect
- LED will flash amber during restart
- LED will turn solid blue when ready
- Total restart time: 60-90 seconds
- All connected devices will automatically reconnect
