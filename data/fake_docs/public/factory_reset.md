# Factory Reset Instructions

## Important Information

**WARNING**: Factory reset will erase all device settings, schedules, and configurations. You will need to set up your device again from scratch.

### What Gets Erased
- WiFi credentials
- Custom device names
- Automation rules and schedules
- User preferences
- Linked account information

### What Is Preserved
- Recorded video (stored in cloud)
- Device warranty registration
- Purchase history

## Before You Reset

1. **Try restarting first** - Many issues can be resolved with a simple restart
2. **Backup settings** - Note any custom configurations you want to restore
3. **Have WiFi password ready** - You'll need it during setup

## SmartRouter Pro & Lite

### Using Reset Button
1. Locate the reset button (small recessed button on back panel)
2. Use a paperclip or pin to press and hold the button
3. Hold for **10 seconds** until LED turns solid red
4. Release button
5. Wait 2-3 minutes for device to reset and restart
6. LED will flash blue when ready for setup

### Using AcmeIoT App
1. Open AcmeIoT Home app
2. Select your router
3. Go to **Settings** > **Advanced**
4. Scroll to bottom and tap **Factory Reset**
5. Enter your account password to confirm
6. Tap **Reset Device**

## SmartPlug

### Using Physical Button
1. Unplug the SmartPlug from wall outlet
2. Press and hold the power button on the plug
3. While holding button, plug back into outlet
4. Continue holding for **7 seconds**
5. LED will flash red three times
6. Release button
7. LED will flash green when ready for setup

### Using AcmeIoT App
1. Open AcmeIoT Home app
2. Select your SmartPlug
3. Go to **Settings** > **Device Management**
4. Tap **Factory Reset**
5. Confirm action

## SmartCam Indoor & Outdoor

### Using Reset Button
1. Locate reset button (on bottom or back of camera)
2. Camera must be powered on
3. Press and hold reset button with paperclip
4. Hold for **15 seconds**
5. LED will flash red rapidly
6. Release when you hear a beep (Indoor) or see LED turn off (Outdoor)
7. Camera will restart and flash blue when ready

### Using AcmeIoT App
1. Open AcmeIoT Home app
2. Select your camera
3. Go to **Settings** > **Camera Management**
4. Tap **Factory Reset Device**
5. Enter password to confirm
6. Tap **Confirm Reset**

## After Reset

### Next Steps
1. Device will enter setup mode automatically
2. Open AcmeIoT Home app
3. Tap **Add Device** (+)
4. Follow on-screen pairing instructions
5. Restore any custom settings

### If Setup Mode Doesn't Activate
- Power cycle the device
- Ensure you held reset button long enough
- Try reset procedure again
