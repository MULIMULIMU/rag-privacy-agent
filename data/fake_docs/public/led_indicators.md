# AcmeIoT Device LED Indicators

## Understanding Your Device Status

All AcmeIoT devices use LED indicators to communicate their current status. This guide helps you understand what each color and pattern means.

## SmartRouter Pro & Lite

### Solid Blue
- Device is powered on and operating normally
- Connected to the internet
- All systems functioning properly

### Flashing Blue (Slow)
- Device is starting up
- Connecting to network
- Normal operation - wait 60-90 seconds

### Flashing Blue (Fast)
- Data transfer in progress
- Firmware update downloading
- Normal operation

### Solid Amber
- No internet connection detected
- Check your ISP connection
- Check cable connections

### Flashing Amber
- Device is restarting
- Configuration mode active
- Temporary state

### Solid Red
- Critical error detected
- Device malfunction
- Contact support if persists beyond 5 minutes

### Flashing Red
- Overheating detected
- Ensure proper ventilation
- Move device to cooler location

## SmartPlug Series

### Solid Green
- Plug is on and delivering power
- Normal operation

### Off (No LED)
- Plug is off (via app or schedule)
- No power being delivered to outlet

### Flashing Green
- Connecting to WiFi network
- Setup mode active

### Solid Amber
- Lost connection to WiFi
- Plug remains in last known state
- Check router connection

## SmartCam Indoor & Outdoor

### Solid Blue
- Camera is online and recording
- Connected to cloud services
- Normal operation

### Flashing Blue
- Camera is in setup mode
- Ready to pair with app

### Solid Red
- No internet connection
- Recording locally only (if SD card present)

### Flashing Red/Blue Alternating
- Firmware update in progress
- Do not unplug during this process

### Off (No LED)
- Privacy mode enabled via app
- Camera is not recording

## Additional Notes

- LED brightness can be adjusted in the AcmeIoT Home app
- LEDs can be disabled for nighttime use (except critical error states)
- If your device shows a pattern not listed here, try restarting it
